## Caveats

- Original full recipe files directly inspected: Herb-Infused-Garlic-Confit-Ghee.txt, Sourdough_Milk_Brioche_Sandwich_Bread_v1_2.pdf, Chocolate_Chunk_Cookies_Recipe.docx.
- Most other source details were recovered from targeted past cooking conversations, including original quantities and user corrections.
- Wife-approved peach cobbler v2.0 differs from milk-containing v1.0; exact peach-skin syrup addition remains unmeasured.
- March and April chocolate-chunk cookie versions were recovered; association to old generic A/B placeholders remains provisional.
- June and October chicken-crust pizza versions have different binders, yields and baking directions.
- Bread formulas remain distinct: commercial-yeast braided brioche, unvalidated sourdough milk brioche, Italian oven loaf, bread machine adaptation, and original May sandwich bread.
- Oxtail braise, six steamed buns, bone rice, and bone miso soup are linked leftover components.
- Genuine unresolved recipes: original homemade tortilla dough; raw-egg coquito processing/coconut can sizes; final carne-asada marinade ratios; final al-pastor pork quesadilla method.
- Three research records are research ideas (Chinese crispy wings, reserved-confit garlic bread, Asian clear-soup investigation), not completed user recipes.
- Earlier v0.3–v1.2 prototype files were referenced but a complete original export was not retrievable; this is not a byte-identical reproduction.
- Nothing was auto-published. Ingredient/method drafts containing unmeasured steps or estimated bridging require owner review.
