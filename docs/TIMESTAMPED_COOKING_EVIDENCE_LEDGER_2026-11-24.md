## Purpose and non-negotiable reconstruction rules
This is a **conversation-derived evidence ledger**, not a rewritten recipe book. A record in `public.recipes` with populated fields is not necessarily a faithful or fully measured recipe.

For every recovered recipe, record (a) original conversation/event date; (b) actual message timestamp, when retrieved; (c) **who stated** the fact; (d) a chronological sequence of real actions, corrections and outcomes; (e) exact quantities versus rough estimates; (f) proposed but unconfirmed actions; (g) version distinctions; and (h) gaps. Never turn a **reply timestamp** into the time a cooking step happened. Never infer elapsed cook time from the distance between messages. Local date and source UTC timestamp can differ; retain offsets when known. Photos prove appearance at capture, not hidden quantities or process.

Confidence labels: **U** = user explicitly reported or confirmed; **A** = assistant-proposed or assistant-authored, user execution unverified; **F** = supplied recipe file; **R** = earlier conversation-recovery summary, original full turn not present. Do not silently turn A or R into U. An estimated amount must remain estimated.
