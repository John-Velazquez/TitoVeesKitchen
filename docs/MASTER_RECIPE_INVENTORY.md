## Status rules

- **Complete source:** Complete recipe directly accessible (currently the herb-infused ghee document).
- **Source document found:** A recipe file exists, but it may be a test batch and not a verified finished outcome.
- **Historical recipe:** Named in an older prototype record; full recipe text must be recovered.
- **Cooking discussion / narration:** Actual cooking was discussed; precise quantities or final recipe may be missing.
- **Concept / research:** Not yet a finished personal recipe; excluded from public recipe publication.

Recipe sources and version notes are stored in the JSON. Keep these notes in the internal catalog rather than showing uncertainty to readers on finished public recipe pages.
